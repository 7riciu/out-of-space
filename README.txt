An astronaut stranded on a mysterious island needs to survive spore storms 🔭🌫️🌱
Befriend slimes and build shelter from the green phenomenon 🛖

How to play:
Collect "calm plants". Feed "calm plants" to slimes 
in order to befriend them. Once friendly, slimes drop 
slime balls. Those can be used in order to build slime 
blocks that shelter you from spore storms.
Spore storms happen every few seconds. Use the in-between 
time to better your protection against the attacks.

Controls:
a - move to the right
d - move to the left
space - jump
e - collect "calm plants", feed slime, place slime blocks
